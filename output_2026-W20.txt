1. Weekly menu:

Monday:  
- Main: Baked Arctic char with mustard-dill sauce, roasted new potatoes, steamed broccoli  
- Vegetarian alt (Person 2): Baked halloumi with mustard-dill sauce, roasted new potatoes, steamed broccoli  

Tuesday (Cook from scratch project):  
- Main: Homemade vegetarian Swedish "kroppkakor" (potato dumplings stuffed with mushrooms and onions), served with lingonberry jam and butter  
- Vegetarian alt: Same  
- Kids get dumplings and lingonberry jam separated  

Wednesday (Soup day):  
- Main: Creamy leek and potato soup with fresh chives, served with rye bread  
- Vegetarian alt: Same  

Thursday:  
- Main: Pan-fried plaice with lemon butter, buttered boiled carrots and peas  
- Vegetarian alt: Pan-fried tofu with lemon butter, buttered boiled carrots and peas  

Friday:  
- Main: Vegetable pasta primavera with fresh peas, asparagus tips, zucchini, tossed with olive oil and Parmesan  
- Vegetarian alt: Same  
- Kids can have pasta and vegetables separated  

2. Shopping list:

Fish & Alternatives:  
- Arctic char fillets (4)  
- Fresh plaice fillets (4)  
- Halloumi (400g)  
- Firm tofu (200g)  

Dairy:  
- Butter (250g)  
- Heavy cream (1 dl)  
- Parmesan cheese (100g)  
- Eggs (6)  

Produce:  
- New potatoes (800g)  
- Broccoli (1 head)  
- Fresh dill (1 bunch)  
- Fresh mustard seeds or prepared mustard (small jar)  
- Lingonberry jam (small jar)  
- Potatoes (1 kg, waxy for kroppkakor)  
- Onions (4 medium)  
- Mushrooms (300g, button or cremini)  
- Leeks (2 large)  
- Fresh chives (1 bunch)  
- Carrots (6 medium)  
- Fresh peas (200g, or frozen)  
- Asparagus tips (150g)  
- Zucchini (2 medium)  
- Lemons (2)  
- Rye bread (for soup)  
- Garlic (1 bulb)  

Pantry:  
- Olive oil  
- All-purpose flour (for kroppkakor dough)  
- Breadcrumbs (for kroppkakor filling)  
- Salt  
- Black pepper  
- Nutmeg (small amount, for kroppkakor)  
- Vegetable broth  

3. Prep plan:

Sunday afternoon:  
- Prepare kroppkakor dough and stuffing, shape dumplings, boil and cool; store covered in fridge  

Monday evening:  
- Bake Arctic char and halloumi with mustard-dill sauce, roast new potatoes, steam broccoli  

Tuesday evening:  
- Reheat kroppkakor by simmering gently, serve with butter and lingonberry jam; keep components separated for kids  

Wednesday evening:  
- Prepare leek and potato soup, blend and season, serve with rye bread  

Thursday evening:  
- Pan-fry plaice and tofu, prepare lemon butter sauce, boil and butter carrots and peas  

Friday evening:  
- Cook pasta, sauté fresh vegetables lightly, toss with olive oil and Parmesan, serve components separated for children  

All meals respect max 45 min cooking (except kroppkakor prepped ahead), use seasonal Swedish produce, meet fish/meat restrictions, avoid coriander for Person 1, and provide vegetarian alternative for Person 2.
