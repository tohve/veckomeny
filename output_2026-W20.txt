1. Weekly menu:

Monday:
- Main: Pan-fried salmon with dill and lemon, boiled new potatoes, steamed green beans
- Vegetarian alt (Person 2): Pan-fried halloumi with dill and lemon, boiled new potatoes, steamed green beans

Tuesday:
- Main: Homemade vegetarian pizza with tomato, mozzarella, mushrooms, and bell peppers (cook from scratch dough)
- Vegetarian alt: Same
- Kids get components separated (toppings on side)

Wednesday:
- Soup day: Creamy root vegetable soup (carrots, parsnip, potato) with whole grain bread
- Vegetarian alt: Same

Thursday:
- Main: Creamy mushroom risotto with parmesan and fresh herbs
- Vegetarian alt: Same

Friday:
- Main: Fish tacos with fried cod, cabbage slaw, avocado, lime crema; tortillas on side for kids
- Vegetarian alt: Fried halloumi tacos with same toppings

2. Shopping list:

Fish:
- Salmon fillets (4)
- Cod fillets (4)

Dairy:
- Halloumi (400g)
- Mozzarella (200g)
- Parmesan cheese (100g)
- Heavy cream (1 dl)
- Butter

Produce:
- New potatoes (800g)
- Fresh dill (1 bunch)
- Lemons (2)
- Green beans (400g)
- Tomatoes (3 medium)
- Mushrooms (300g)
- Bell peppers (2)
- Carrots (4)
- Parsnip (2)
- Potatoes (2 large)
- Fresh herbs (parsley, chives)
- Cabbage (small, for slaw)
- Avocado (1)
- Lime (1)
- Onions (2)
- Garlic (1 bulb)

Pantry:
- Olive oil
- Flour (for pizza dough)
- Active dry yeast
- Sugar (small amount for dough)
- Vegetable broth
- Arborio rice (for risotto)
- Tortillas (8 small, corn or flour)
- Spices: salt, black pepper, paprika
- Mayonnaise (for lime crema)

Bread:
- Whole grain bread (for soup)

3. Prep plan:

Sunday evening or Monday morning:
- Prepare pizza dough, allow to rise; store refrigerated

Monday evening:
- Pan-fry salmon and halloumi, boil new potatoes, steam beans

Tuesday evening:
- Roll out pizza dough, top with tomato sauce, mozzarella, mushrooms, bell peppers (kids get toppings separately), bake

Wednesday evening:
- Prepare creamy root vegetable soup; serve with whole grain bread

Thursday evening:
- Make mushroom risotto, stirring frequently for 25-30 mins

Friday evening:
- Fry cod and halloumi strips, prepare cabbage slaw with lime crema, warm tortillas, assemble tacos; serve components separated for kids
